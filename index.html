Schedule Change Policy

If players agree to reschedule a match, it is the responsibility of the player/team listed first involved to update both TeamReach and the FSR Portal.

TeamReach should be updated to notify opposing player/team of the schedule change.

The existing reserved court on the FSR Portal needs to be canceled and then reserve with the court with the date and time of the rescheduled match. Let the player/team that the rescheduled match has been completed on FSR Portal and TeamReach.

A match rescheduling is not considered complete until both TeamReach and the FSR Portal have been updated.
